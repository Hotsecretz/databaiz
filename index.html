GazetteLandHigh Related: 2 earlier leads → Vol. CXXVIII No. 61 · 6 Aug 2026

State moves to compulsorily acquire 214 parcels in Nakuru for bypass construction

A notice under the Land Act lists 214 parcels earmarked for compulsory acquisition along the proposed eastern bypass. Owners have 30 days to submit claims. Several listed parcels sit inside a settlement scheme disputed in 2024.

Verbatim extract
"…the National Land Commission intends to acquire the parcels of land described in the Schedule for the construction of the Nakuru Eastern Bypass…" ✓ verified in document · Page 4,102, paragraph 2 · copied, not paraphrased
Suggested angle
Breaking: who is affected and what the 30-day window means
Names, parcel counts and the claims deadline, with the notice as the document of record. Practical detail for affected owners belongs in the story, not just the announcement.
More angles: investigative, longform, solutions
  • Investigative: cross-reference parcel numbers against the 2024 settlement dispute. Any transfers shortly before gazettement?
  • Longform: what did owners on the last bypass actually receive, and how long did it take?
  • Solutions: a claims guide for affected owners, with deadlines and free legal help.
Follow-up questions
  • Request the full parcel schedule from the NLC and match against the land registry.
  • Which valuers were appointed, and by whom?
  • Has the environmental licence been issued?
Ethics & legal flags

The notice names individual parcel owners. Publishing names is lawful (public document) but weigh fairness before naming people not yet contacted. No court order restricts reporting.

Welcome to DatabAIz

The tool reads Kenya's official record every morning and turns it into story leads. Your job takes three moves:

1
Read a card
Each lead has a summary, an exact quote already verified against the document, the legal flags, and a link to the original. Amber panels on the left show which sources are healthy.
2
Climb the ladder
Levels 1–2 were checked automatically. Open levels 3, 4 and 5 on the right of a card and tick them as you verify. The glowing level on the first card is where to start.
3
Decide
Docket it, Hold it, Dismiss it, or, once all five levels clear, Assign it to a reporter. Docketed items land in the Docket tab. Nothing can be assigned unverified: that is the whole point.
